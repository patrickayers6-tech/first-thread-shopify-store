## Page: Our Story

### Small projects. Real finishes.

First Thread Studio is being built for adults who like the idea of making something by hand but do not want a second job disguised as a hobby.

Traditional needlepoint can begin with a trip through canvases, mesh sizes, thread conversions, finishing services, and instructions written for people who already know the vocabulary. We are designing a smaller first step: one original mini project, one core stitch, pre-sorted color, and calm help when the thread tangles or a row needs to come out.

The founding designs are original to First Thread Studio. Current product images are labeled digital visualizations while the production-format samples are tested. We will not open checkout until a true novice can complete the physical kit without live coaching, the thread quantities and print registration pass, and a real fulfillment date is available.

That is the whole idea: make the first finish feel possible—and make every promise earn its place on the box.

## Page: How It Works

### Your first five stitches

**1. Open only what you need.**  
Each planned kit keeps the canvas, color cards, tools, and first-step sequence together.

**2. Match the color.**  
The printed canvas shows where each pre-sorted thread belongs. You do not need to choose a palette or translate a chart before starting.

**3. Learn one stitch.**  
The core project uses a single diagonal tent stitch. Optional texture does not appear until the basic motion is comfortable.

**4. Recover calmly.**  
The planned quick-start and original video help show how to undo a stitch, restart a thread, and relax over-tight tension.

**5. Finish it yourself.**  
The small format is intended for a patch, tag, ornament, coaster, or mini display without an expensive professional finishing service.

### What is planned inside

- one original printed 10–13 mesh canvas;
- pre-sorted cotton or wool thread with learning-room overage;
- one blunt tapestry needle;
- one needle threader;
- illustrated quick-start card;
- original video help;
- project-specific finishing guidance.

Final materials, dimensions, completion range, and finishing method will be confirmed from the approved physical sample.

## Page: Beginner Help

### Start here when something feels wrong

**My thread will not go through the needle.**  
Flatten and trim the thread end, place it through the included threader, then pull the threader—not the loose thread—through the eye.

**My stitches look uneven.**  
Let the thread rest on the canvas instead of pulling it tight. The goal is consistent coverage, not maximum tension.

**I stitched the wrong color.**  
Stop when you notice it. Use the blunt needle to lift the last stitch gently and reverse the same path. Do not cut through the canvas.

**The canvas is bending.**  
Tension is probably too tight. Loosen the next rows and rotate the project as directed in the final guide. The approved sample test will determine whether any optional support is needed.

**I lost the needle or threader.**  
The planned Stitch Rescue Pack provides a compatible blunt needle, threader, and matching thread reserve. It will be offered only after component review.

**I need human help.**  
Email `firstthread-studio@agentmail.to` with the design name, a clear photo, and where you became stuck. Response-time language will be published before checkout opens.

## Page: FAQ

### Is this really for someone who has never stitched?
That is the design goal. We will publish the exact completion range and approved format only after a true novice finishes the production sample without live coaching.

### What is needlepoint?
Needlepoint covers an open mesh canvas with thread. The founding projects are built around a single diagonal tent stitch so a new stitcher can make visible progress without learning a library of techniques.

### Are the current product images photographs?
No. They are original digital product visualizations and are labeled as such. Real sample photography will be added before checkout opens.

### Why is checkout closed?
We will not accept payment until the physical sample, novice test, thread quantity, print alignment, landed cost, and fulfillment date are verified.

### Does the kit contain a needle?
Yes. The planned kit contains a blunt tapestry needle and is positioned for adult use. Final warnings and component details follow the production sample and supplier review.

### How long will a kit take?
No exact time has been published. The design target is a small project completed across a few calm sessions; the real range will come from the novice test.

### Will I need professional finishing?
The founding format is specifically intended to avoid it. The final self-finishing method will be published with each approved sample.

### Can I buy the patterns separately?
Not at launch. The first validation focuses on complete kits so the material quantities and learning experience can be tested together.

### Do you ship outside the United States?
The founding drop is planned for U.S. addresses only. Additional markets will be evaluated after actual shipping cost, customs, and delivery performance are known.

## Page: Shipping & Returns

### Shipping area

The founding drop is planned for delivery within the United States only. Carrier, service level, order cutoff, processing time, and delivery estimates will be published after fulfillment testing.

### Order processing

No processing-time promise is active while checkout is closed. Before launch, the store will state whether each product is in stock or a preorder and will provide an exact processing or shipment window.

### Address changes

Contact `firstthread-studio@agentmail.to` as soon as possible. An address cannot be changed after the carrier accepts the package.

### Damaged, incomplete, or incorrect orders

Email within 14 days of delivery with the order number and clear photos of the package and contents. We will replace the affected component or kit, or provide an appropriate refund, after verifying the issue.

### Unused returns

The planned policy allows returns of unused, unstitched, complete kits in original packaging within 30 days of delivery. The customer is responsible for return shipping unless the order was incorrect or defective. The exact return address and instructions will be provided by customer service.

### Opened or started kits

Because thread is cut and materials are consumed during use, started kits are not eligible for a preference-based return. Defects, missing materials, and quantity problems remain covered; contact us so the actual problem can be corrected.

### Refund timing

Approved refunds will be returned to the original payment method. Bank posting time is controlled by the payment provider. Exact processing language will be aligned with Shopify Payments before launch.
