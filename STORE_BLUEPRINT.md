# First Thread Studio — Store Blueprint and Launch Controls

**Build:** Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme based on Shopify's MIT-licensed Dawn architecture, with an original First Thread Studio design layer, original product concepts, original copy, and original product visualizations.

## Reference model

The October 2026 benchmark ranked **TobioShop first** for the strongest current hero-product discipline and paid-creative momentum in the selected compact beginner craft category. This store adapts only general business patterns:

- one visually demonstrable hero project;
- a narrow good/better offer ladder;
- an inexpensive troubleshooting add-on;
- tactile process-led merchandising.

It also borrows non-protectable operating patterns from The Woobles (true-beginner onboarding), Pipsticks (drop cadence), Sculpd (occasion merchandising), and Diamond Art Club (structured project facts).

**Clean-room boundary:** no names, logos, art, characters, product photography, layouts, packaging, instructions, trade dress, proprietary materials, or exact copy were copied.

## Primary surface

**Decide / Learn**, with a secondary **Explore** catalog. The home page must first convince an adult non-crafter that one small project is finishable, then allow comparison among only three founding designs.

## Brand system

- **Working name:** First Thread Studio
- **Tagline:** Small projects. Real finishes.
- **Promise:** A first needlepoint project with the materials, sequence, and recovery help organized before the box closes.
- **Voice:** warm, plainspoken, specific, encouraging, never precious.
- **Palette:** paper `#F4EEDF`, cream `#FFF9ED`, midnight `#16263B`, poppy `#D85143`, mustard `#E4AC42`, sage `#55796D`, dusty blue `#5872A8`.
- **Type posture:** editorial Georgia headings with practical Trebuchet body copy.
- **Design posture:** tactile printed-paper composition, visible borders, purposeful asymmetry, no gradients, no glass effects, no fabricated statistics or reviews.

## Search and Shopify Catalog data

Every product record includes literal fields for:

- finished dimensions;
- mesh count;
- thread material and number of colors;
- prior experience;
- core stitch;
- finishing method;
- age/safety positioning;
- preorder/launch status;
- original-design provenance;
- exact planned contents.

This supports human search, Shopify Catalog, Shop, and AI shopping surfaces without depending on poetic copy alone.

## Launch gates

Checkout stays closed until every item below is verified:

1. Production-format physical sample exists.
2. A true novice completes it without live coaching.
3. Print registration and thread quantity pass.
4. Complete landed cost is **$11 or less**.
5. Exact preorder or in-stock ship date is published.
6. Fulfillment capacity and replacement buffer are defined.
7. Product photography includes the real sample.
8. Working name receives an attorney-level trademark clearance or is replaced.
9. Legal business identity, address, tax settings, and return address are entered by the owner.
10. Test order, refund, cancellation, mobile checkout, email receipt, and tax behavior are verified in Shopify.

## Image policy

The current product images are original, deterministic digital visualizations. Each hero file visibly states: **“Digital product visualization · final materials may vary.”** Product alt text repeats the disclosure. Replace or supplement these images with real sample photography before payment is enabled.

## Anti-fabrication rules

- No reviews or testimonials until they exist.
- No sales counters, inventory scarcity, countdowns, or customer totals.
- No exact completion-time claim until novice testing.
- No “free shipping” claim except the planned $69 duo, and only after shipping economics are confirmed.
- No preorder opening without a dated fulfillment window.

## Slop diagnostic

**Target score: 0/10.**

- no tech gradient;
- no generic violet accent;
- no equal-weight icon feature tiles;
- no accent rails;
- no glass blur;
- no decorative monument metrics;
- no icon toppers;
- hero is appropriate for the Decide/Learn surface;
- editorial type selected intentionally;
- catalog and process sections use distinct compositions.

## Account-dependent setup checklist

The theme, product CSV, copy, imagery, and static live preview do not require an API. The following actions require a Shopify owner account:

- create the store and choose the `myshopify.com` handle;
- upload the theme ZIP;
- import products;
- create navigation menus and pages;
- configure sender email, payments, taxes, markets, shipping, and policies;
- choose or connect a domain;
- remove storefront password or select a paid plan.

Never request or paste a Shopify password or payment card in chat. Use the browser vault for credentials and Shopify's secure billing form.
