Shipping & returns.

Pre-launch draft: no processing-time promise is active while checkout is closed.

Shipping area

The founding drop is planned for delivery within the United States only. Carrier, service level, order cutoff, and delivery estimates will be published after fulfillment testing.

Damaged, incomplete, or incorrect orders

Email within 14 days of delivery with the order number and clear photos. We will replace the affected component or kit, or provide an appropriate refund, after verifying the issue.

Unused returns

The planned policy allows returns of unused, unstitched, complete kits in original packaging within 30 days of delivery. The customer is responsible for return shipping unless the order was incorrect or defective.

Opened or started kits

Started kits are not eligible for a preference-based return. Defects, missing materials, and quantity problems remain covered.
