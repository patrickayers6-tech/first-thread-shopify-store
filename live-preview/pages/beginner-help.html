Start here when something feels wrong.

My thread will not go through the needle.

Flatten and trim the thread end, place it through the included threader, then pull the threader—not the loose thread—through the eye.

My stitches look uneven.

Let the thread rest on the canvas instead of pulling it tight. The goal is consistent coverage, not maximum tension.

I stitched the wrong color.

Stop when you notice it. Use the blunt needle to lift the last stitch gently and reverse the same path. Do not cut through the canvas.

The canvas is bending.

Tension is probably too tight. Loosen the next rows. The approved sample test will determine whether optional support is needed.

I need human help.

Email firstthread-studio@agentmail.to with the design name, a clear photo, and where you became stuck.
