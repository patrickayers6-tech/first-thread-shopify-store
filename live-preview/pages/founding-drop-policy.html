Proof before payment.

First Thread Studio will not accept a founding-drop payment until the product page states the approved materials and contents, shows a real production sample, gives the novice-tested completion range, and publishes a clear shipment date or window.

If the founding drop uses preorders

  1. The product page will clearly say Preorder before the add-to-cart button.
  2. The expected shipment date or window will be shown before payment.
  3. Customers may cancel for a full refund any time before fulfillment by contacting firstthread-studio@agentmail.to.
  4. If the shipment date materially changes, affected customers will receive the new date and a simple cancel-for-refund option.
  5. A preorder will never be represented as in-stock inventory.
