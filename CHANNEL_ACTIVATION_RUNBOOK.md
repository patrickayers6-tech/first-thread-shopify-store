# Channel Activation Runbook

This is the exact operational sequence for turning the verified prelaunch build into a sellable omnichannel business. Shopify remains the inventory, price, product-data, and order system of record.

## Gate 0 — product truth

Do not publish purchasable listings until all are complete:

- production-format sample and registration QC;
- true-novice completion test;
- verified materials, dimensions, contents, weight, and country of origin;
- thread/component quantities and replacement buffer;
- landed cost at or below the approved ceiling;
- product-liability review/insurance;
- real sample photography;
- final packaging and labeling;
- fulfillment capacity, return address, and dated shipping promise;
- brand/name clearance proportionate to launch risk.

## Gate 1 — Shopify owner setup

Owner-only inputs:

- legal business name and address;
- tax identity/settings;
- payout bank and Shopify Payments approval;
- paid plan/billing approval;
- custom domain approval;
- acceptance of Shopify and channel supplemental terms.

Agent-executable after secure login:

1. Push theme as **unpublished**.
2. Import five products as **draft**, unpublished, zero inventory.
3. Read back exact product count, title, handle, status, price, category, and inventory.
4. Create collections, pages, menus, policies, metafield definitions, and Knowledge Base FAQs.
5. Install Shopify Forms, Email, Flow, Search & Discovery, Bundles, Inbox, Shop, and official channel apps.
6. Keep storefront password/checkout lock until Gate 0 is complete.

## Gate 2 — AI and search discovery

1. Complete title, description, standardized category, vendor, price, availability, images, alt text, variants, weight, material, dimensions, shipping, and return data.
2. Publish only approved products to Online Store.
3. In **Sales channels > Agentic**, keep Shopify Catalog access enabled for desired channels.
4. Validate `/agents.md`, `/llms.txt`, and `/llms-full.txt`.
5. Enter approved FAQs in Shopify Knowledge Base.
6. Use Agentic search preview to test natural-language queries such as:
   - beginner needlepoint kit for an adult who has never stitched;
   - small screen-free craft gift under $50;
   - compact craft-night activity for two;
   - easy botanical needlepoint project;
   - first needlepoint kit with pre-sorted thread.
7. Inspect listing-quality feedback; improve factual completeness only.
8. Never invent reviews, ratings, identifiers, popularity, inventory, or completion times.

## Gate 3 — DTC and native channels

1. Connect custom `.com` domain and verify SSL.
2. Configure shipping zones/rates, taxes, Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and notifications.
3. Run test order, fulfillment, cancellation, refund, and failed-payment flows.
4. Activate Shop, Agentic, Google & YouTube, then Pinterest.
5. Verify product count, prices, stock, links, and image approvals in every channel.
6. Activate Meta and TikTok only after each business account passes its review.

## Gate 4 — marketplaces

1. Apply for GTIN/UPC through GS1 or platform exemptions as appropriate. Never fabricate identifiers.
2. Create/approve seller accounts for Etsy, Amazon, eBay, Walmart; Target Plus is application-only.
3. Use Marketplace Connect for Amazon/eBay/Walmart/Target Plus where supported.
4. Use channel-specific titles, bullets, attributes, images, price rules, shipping profiles, and returns.
5. Import orders into Shopify; keep one inventory location as source of truth.
6. Reconcile each channel's exact active SKU count before enabling ads.

## Gate 5 — tracking and retention

1. Configure Shopify Customer Privacy before nonessential tracking.
2. Connect GA4 through Google & YouTube; connect Meta/TikTok/Pinterest/Microsoft native pixels/APIs once each—no duplicate tags.
3. Start Shopify Email flows: waitlist welcome, launch, abandoned checkout, post-purchase, review request, winback.
4. Add Judge.me only after real fulfillment so all reviews are verified and traceable.
5. Weekly dashboard: revenue, contribution margin, CAC, CVR, AOV, channel stock, returns, support SLA, agentic search queries, and listing-quality issues.

## Launch order recommended for this brand

`Shopify DTC → Shop/Agentic → Google free listings → Pinterest → Instagram/Facebook → TikTok Shop → Etsy → Amazon → eBay/Walmart → Faire/wholesale → Target Plus`

Do not activate all channels simultaneously. Master one profitable, operationally stable channel before the next marketplace launch.
